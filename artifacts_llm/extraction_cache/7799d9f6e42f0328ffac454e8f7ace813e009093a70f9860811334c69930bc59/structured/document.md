## Margins expand on lower input cost

Tata Power Company Limited generates and supplies electricity. It constructs and operates independent power plants, as well as specialises in transmission and distribution of electricity.

- In Q1FY24, consolidated revenue increased 5.0% YoY to Rs. 15,213cr, supported by moderate growth in the transmission and distribution business.
- EBITDA margin rose 850 bps YoY to 21.1%, driven by a decrease in prices of solar cells, modules and wafers. As a result, reported PAT grew 22.4% YoY to Rs. 973cr.
- Capacity expansions, improvement in execution capabilities, and corrections in input prices make the business prospects for the company look promising. Hence, we expect margin improvement to continue, and upgrade our rating on the stock to BUY with a revised target price of Rs. 301 based on SOTP valuation.

## Moderate growth in revenue

Consolidated revenue grew 5.0% YoY to Rs. 15,213cr in Q1FY24, led by growth of 3.6% YoY in the transmission and distribution business. The Odisha business stabilised on the back of various restoration and field activities to overcome uncertain weather conditions. The generation business grew a significant 67.5% on a QoQ basis due to improved cost control and better efficiency. The Mundra plant aided performance during the quarter and will continue to operate until 30 September 2023. Its operations may be extended due to heat conditions. In the electric vehicle (EV) business, the company added 4,400 public chargers and 50,000 home chargers and plans to add about 1,200 bus chargers across various cities.

## Strong improvement in margins

EBITDA surged 76.0% YoY to Rs. 3,215cr in Q1FY24 on account of a drastic reduction in prices of solar cells, modules and wafers. Hence, EBITDA margin expanded 850 bps YoY to 21.1%. Consolidated PAT grew 22.4% YoY to Rs. 973cr, supported by income from exceptional items (Rs. 235cr).

## Key highlights

- In Q1FY24, a capacity addition of 5.8 GW brought the total installed capacity to ~422GW, of which 77% was attributable to renewable energy.
- The company signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the Maharashtra government of Rs. 13,000cr for pumped hydro storage projects. This project will support green energy landscape and generate employment for over 6,000 people.
- Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited signed power purchase agreements for commissioning two solar projects (200MW and 150MW) to provide clean and sustainable electricity to MSEDCL.

## Valuation

Owing to extreme weather conditions in the country, consumption of power was lower than expected in the quarter. However, the company recorded healthy performance on account of lower input costs. Capacity additions in the renewable energy segment, corrections in polysilicon and thermal coal prices, strong execution of business, and large capex will aid the company in achieving robust operational and financial performance. Thus, we upgrade our rating on the stock to BUY with a revised target price of Rs. 301 based on SOTP valuation.
