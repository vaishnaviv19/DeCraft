How it works

  1. Read. Layout, fonts, colours, tables and charts are extracted from each PDF.
  2. Understand. Totals, margins, growth and repeated figures are found in the data.
  3. Generate. New numbers that keep those rules, kept well away from the real ones.
  4. Rebuild. Every block goes back where it was, and 31 quality checks verify the result.
